### Summary
  A frictionless online whiteboard for instant drawing and sharing.

### Details
  Witeboard is designed to get out of the way: open the site, start drawing anywhere on the canvas, and share the board link with others for quick real-time collaboration. An account is not required for basic use. Optional sign-in, including Google sign-in, lets users keep past boards across devices and stay up to date with new features. The interface also includes simple feedback and help options for quick improvements.

### Website
  [https://witeboard.com/](https://witeboard.com/)

### What is it?
  Witeboard is a minimal online whiteboard that removes setup friction so individuals and groups can sketch and share instantly.

### How to use it?
  Go to witeboard.com, draw on the canvas right away, copy the board link to invite collaborators, and sign up only if you want to save boards across devices.

### Key Features
  No login required, instant canvas, simple drawing tools, shareable board links, real-time collaboration, and optional accounts for saved boards.

### Use Cases
  Quick explanations during calls, ad-hoc brainstorming, tutoring, lightweight interviews, and spontaneous sketching with a shared link.

### FAQ
  Do I need an account to use Witeboard? No, you can draw and share without signing up. Why create an account? An account lets you see your past boards across devices.
