### Summary
Built for agile teams, Metro Retro provides templates and facilitator controls designed to drive engaging retrospectives and planning sessions.

### Details
Metro Retro offers structured templates (sailboat, Start/Stop, Fishbone), privacy/anonymous input, voting, Jira integration, board history, and playful features like confetti cannon. 

### Website URL
https://metroretro.io

### What is it?
A collaborative whiteboard platform optimized for agile ceremonies (retrospectives, planning, estimation), combining visual boards with facilitator tools. 

### How to use it
Sign up, create or choose a template, invite team members, optionally use private writing mode, vote on ideas, track action items, export results or import CSV. 

### Key features
- Agile‑focused templates (retro, planning, estimation)
- Facilitator controls: timers, voting, anonymity, confetti
- Team spaces, Jira integration, task tracking
- Board history & import/export
- GDPR compliant and secure collaboration 

### Use cases
- Sprint retrospectives and planning ceremonies
- Team alignment workshops or strategy sessions
- Estimation and backlog mapping
- Lightweight project kickoff or brainstorming

### FAQ
**Q**: Is there a free version or trial?  
**A**: Yes—free trial for 30 days; afterwards boards remain accessible in read-only mode.   
**Q**: Can participants be anonymous?  
**A**: Yes—private writing lets team members submit input anonymously before group sharing.
