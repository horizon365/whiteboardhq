### Summary
  A visual collaboration platform with boards and a digital sandbox canvas.

### Details
  Padlet provides shared spaces where groups can collect, organize, and share ideas visually. Its board format is widely used in classrooms and creative projects, and its Sandbox feature adds a freeform digital canvas for whiteboarding, lessons, and live activities. The platform reports around 40 million users and is rated highly across review and app stores. Creating a board or sandbox is free, and accounts are quick to set up.

### Website
  [https://padlet.com/](https://padlet.com/)

### What is it?
  Padlet is an online visual collaboration platform for gathering and arranging ideas on boards and freeform canvas spaces.

### How to use it?
  Sign up for free, create a board or Sandbox, add notes, images, and other content, then share the link so participants can contribute together.

### Key Features
  Collaborative boards for collecting and organizing ideas, Sandbox digital canvas, media-rich posts, simple link sharing, education-friendly workflows, and free board creation.

### Use Cases
  Classroom activities and lessons, team brainstorming, project idea collection, mood boards, event planning, and community collaboration.

### FAQ
  Is Padlet free? Yes, you can create boards and sandboxes for free, with paid plans for additional features. Is it suitable for schools? Yes, Padlet is widely used by educators and students.
