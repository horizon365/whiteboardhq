### Summary
  An online whiteboard built for smooth sketching and team collaboration.

### Details
  Limnu focuses on a natural drawing experience, aiming to feel as easy as picking up a traditional marker. Boards have no edge boundaries, so teams can sketch, diagram, and brainstorm without running out of room. It supports group collaboration, easy sharing, board organization for searching and sorting, and saving boards for later. Limnu runs in modern web browsers and also offers an iOS app. Team plans add security and admin controls for managing who can access, share, and invite.

### Website
  [https://limnu.com/](https://limnu.com/)

### What is it?
  Limnu is an online whiteboard for sketching ideas with other people, designed around a realistic and responsive drawing feel.

### How to use it?
  Open Limnu and start sketching right away without signing up, then invite teammates to the board and share it with anyone for collaboration.

### Key Features
  Realistic marker-like drawing, infinite board space, real-time group collaboration, link sharing, board organization, saved boards, browser and iOS access, plus team security and admin controls.

### Use Cases
  Remote tutoring and math collaboration, business process brainstorming, engineering diagramming, design reviews with clients, and team brainstorming sessions.

### FAQ
  Can I try Limnu without an account? Yes, you can start sketching right away with no signup. Is there a paid plan? Yes, team plans are priced per member with a free trial available.
