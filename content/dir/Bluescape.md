### Summary
  A secure visual collaboration workspace for large, complex teams.

### Details
  Bluescape is built for organizations that need a shared visual space at scale. Teams can place documents, images, video, live streams, and data from preferred apps on a large workspace, then work on them together in real time. It is positioned for security-sensitive environments, with authorization to operate at FedRAMP Moderate and by the U.S. DoD at IL4/IL5 for controlled unclassified information. It is used in media and entertainment, security operations, government and defense, emergency response, and architecture and design.

### Website
  [https://www.bluescape.com/](https://www.bluescape.com/)

### What is it?
  Bluescape is a secure, scalable visual collaboration workspace where distributed teams and AI agents can view, discuss, and act on content together.

### How to use it?
  Request a demo or set up a workspace, then create canvases, add documents, images, video, and app content, invite team members, and collaborate in real time.

### Key Features
  Expansive shared canvas, real-time collaboration at scale, support for documents, images, video, live streams, and external apps, workflow orchestration, and enterprise-grade security including FedRAMP and DoD authorizations.

### Use Cases
  Security operations and incident response, government and defense planning, media production reviews, architecture and design coordination, and large stakeholder workshops.

### FAQ
  Is Bluescape aimed at individuals? No, it is primarily an enterprise platform for large and security-conscious organizations. Does it support real-time collaboration? Yes, large groups can interact with content and workflows together in real time.
