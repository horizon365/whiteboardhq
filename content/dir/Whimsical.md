### Summary
  A visual workspace for diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, and wireframes.

### Details
  Whimsical provides an infinite canvas aimed at product builders who need to capture and share ideas quickly. Core building blocks include diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, and wireframes, along with AI features that can generate diagrams and organize information. Teams can edit together in real time, leave focused comments, use expert-made templates, embed images and videos from services such as Loom and YouTube, and restore earlier versions from version history.

### Website
  [https://whimsical.com/](https://whimsical.com/)

### What is it?
  Whimsical is a collaborative online whiteboard and diagramming tool for designing, documenting, and communicating product ideas visually.

### How to use it?
  Sign up for free, create a board, then choose a diagram, flowchart, mind map, or wireframe and invite teammates to edit and comment in real time.

### Key Features
  Infinite canvas, diagrams and flowcharts, mind maps, wireframes, real-time multiplayer editing, comments, templates, image and video embeds, version history, and AI-assisted creation.

### Use Cases
  Product planning and implementation diagrams, UX wireframing, mind mapping ideas, team brainstorming, and documenting flows for stakeholders.

### FAQ
  Is Whimsical free? Yes, there is a free plan with limits on objects created per month, and paid plans unlock higher limits. Can my team edit together? Yes, boards support real-time multiplayer editing.
