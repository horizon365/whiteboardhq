### Summary
  A collaborative online whiteboard for planning and diagramming.

### Details
  Sketchboard is aimed at distributed teams that need more structure than a blank canvas. It combines freehand sketching with sketch-style shapes, automated connections, sticky notes, and markdown notes on an infinite canvas. Team members see each other's movements and cursors in real time, and a comment box doubles as a mini chat for discussing ideas. Boards can be reused for presentations, and integrations connect the tool with Microsoft Teams, Slack, GitHub, Webex, and Google Drive.

### Website
  [https://sketchboard.io/](https://sketchboard.io/)

### What is it?
  Sketchboard is an online collaborative whiteboard and shared sketching space for planning, diagramming, and remote teamwork.

### How to use it?
  Sign up for a free account, create a board, sketch with freehand tools or sketch shapes, add notes and connections, then share the board link so teammates can collaborate live.

### Key Features
  Infinite canvas, over 400 sketch shapes with automated connections, sticky notes and markdown notes, live cursors and real-time updates, board reuse for presentations, and integrations with Teams, Slack, GitHub, Webex, and Google Drive.

### Use Cases
  Software design and UML diagrams, sprint retrospectives, remote planning sessions, UX flow sketching, mind mapping, and team presentations.

### FAQ
  Is Sketchboard free? Yes, the free plan includes up to 3 editable boards and 5 users. Can several people draw at once? Yes, multiple users can edit the same board simultaneously with live updates.
